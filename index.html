Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Chennai Transit Network Builder is a mobile game designed to let players build and manage a fictional transit network inspired by Chennai.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

1. Personal Information

Chennai Transit Network Builder does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or other personally identifiable information.

2. Game Data

Game information such as scores, settings, progress, or other gameplay preferences may be stored locally on your device.

This information is used only to provide the game's functionality and improve your gameplay experience.

3. In-App Purchases

Chennai Transit Network Builder may offer optional in-app purchases.

Purchases are processed through Google Play. The developer does not directly receive or store your payment card or banking information.

The application may use RevenueCat to manage purchases, subscriptions, purchase validation, and access to purchased features.

RevenueCat may process information related to purchases and app installations as necessary to provide these services.

For additional information about how these services handle data, please refer to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

4. Advertising

Chennai Transit Network Builder does not currently display third-party advertisements.

5. Location Information

Although the game is inspired by Chennai and may include fictional or simplified representations of Chennai transit stations, the application does not require or collect your device's precise location.

6. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell users' personal information.

Limited technical or purchase-related information may be processed by third-party service providers such as Google Play and RevenueCat when required to provide in-app purchasing functionality.

7. Children's Privacy

Chennai Transit Network Builder does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If the application is made available to children, any third-party services used by the application will be configured in accordance with applicable Google Play policies.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information handled through the application. However, no electronic system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's features or third-party services change.

Any changes will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly.
