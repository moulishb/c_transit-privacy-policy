Cambridge Events

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Cambridge Events is an Android application designed to help people discover events, meetups and activities in Cambridge, United Kingdom.

We respect your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

The initial version of Cambridge Events does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, telephone number or email address.

We do not intentionally collect personal information directly through the app.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Cambridge Events may store preferences and saved events locally on your device to provide features such as favourites.

This information is used for app functionality and is not transmitted to our servers by these local storage features.

3. Analytics and Advertising

The initial version of Cambridge Events does not integrate third-party advertising networks, behavioural tracking tools or analytics SDKs.

If these technologies are introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated to explain what information is processed and why.

4. Event Information and External Websites

Cambridge Events displays event information and may provide links to external websites operated by event organisers or ticket providers.

When you open an external website, its own privacy policy and data handling practices apply. We recommend reviewing those policies before providing personal information.

5. How We Use Information

Any information stored by the application is used to support features such as event discovery, navigation, filtering and saved events.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. The initial version does not transmit locally saved favourites to our servers.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally saved preferences and favourites may remain on your device until you clear the application's data or uninstall the application, subject to your device's backup and restore settings.

Because the initial version does not provide user accounts, there is no account deletion process.

8. Children's Privacy

Cambridge Events is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to design and maintain the application securely. However, no software or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy as the application develops or its functionality changes. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
